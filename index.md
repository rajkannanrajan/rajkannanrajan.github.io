## About Me

I am a Vice President and Lead Business Analyst at a global investment banking Global Capability Center in Chennai, bringing 20 years of professional experience across wholesale lending, enterprise data architecture, and digital transformation. Alongside my technology delivery leadership, I serve as Co-Chair for Campus Relationships and Academic Engagement and Site Co-Lead for the Early Careers Program.

My academic research focuses on **corporate governance, board-level oversight of artificial intelligence, Model Risk Management (MRM), regulatory compliance, and corporate sustainability reporting (SEBI BRSR)**. I also serve as an Industry Expert on the Board of Studies for the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SSN College of Engineering.

I hold an M.Sc. in Data Science from Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education (2026), an M.A. in Economics from Manonmaniam Sundaranar University (2017), and a B.Tech. in Information Technology from Anna University (2006).
