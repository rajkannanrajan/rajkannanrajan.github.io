## About Me

I am a Vice President and Lead Business Analyst at a global investment banking Global Capability Center in Chennai, with 20 years of industry experience specializing in wholesale lending, enterprise data architecture, and digital transformation. Within the institution, I also serve as Co-Chair for Campus Relationships and Academic Engagement and Site Co-Lead for the Early Careers Program.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **corporate governance, board-level oversight of artificial intelligence, algorithmic & model risk management (MRM), and corporate sustainability reporting (BRSR)**. In addition to my industry leadership, I serve as an Industry Expert on the Board of Studies for the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at SSN College of Engineering, Chennai.

## Education

- **M.Sc. in Data Science**, Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education, 2023 – 2026
- **M.A. in Economics**, Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, 2013 – 2017
- **B.Tech. in Information Technology**, Anna University, 2002 – 2006

## Research Interests

- **Board-Level AI & Algorithmic Governance:** Director technological literacy, algorithmic risk oversight, Model Risk Management (MRM), and multi-jurisdictional regulatory frameworks (RBI, EU AI Act, global standards).
- **Corporate Sustainability & ESG Disclosures:** Evolution of SEBI’s Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) framework and corporate governance efficacy.
- **Enterprise Financial Architecture & Capital Markets:** Wholesale lending platforms (Oracle FLEXCUBE, Finastra Loan IQ, FIS ACBS, Murex MX.3), Open Data Mesh specifications, metadata governance, and Indian credit derivatives regulation.
