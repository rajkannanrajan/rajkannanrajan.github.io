## About Me

Seasoned banking and financial services technology leader with nearly two decades of experience driving business and digital transformation at global institutions including Bank of America Merrill Lynch, Société Générale, and Barclays. Proven expertise spans capital markets, regulatory compliance, data management, and sustainable finance, with a consistent focus on translating complex strategies into measurable business outcomes. Demonstrated success in leading large-scale programs and establishing scalable Centers of Excellence within Global Capability Centers (GCCs) anchored in innovation, operational excellence, and data-driven decision-making. Equally dedicated to organizational growth and talent development, combining the ability to build high-performing teams with a deep commitment to nurturing early-career professionals from campus recruitment through long-term career progression.
